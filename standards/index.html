Standards

Language Bridge separates official Texas standards — the English Language Proficiency Standards (19 TAC Ch. 120) and the TEKS for English and Spanish Language Arts (Ch. 110 / Ch. 128) — from local instructional frameworks. Every correlation states, in one sentence, why an activity addresses the standard. Nothing is attached that cannot be justified.

Plain-language summaries are paraphrases for teacher display, verified against the codified Texas Administrative Code. See each activity's Teacher view for the full correlation and rationale.
