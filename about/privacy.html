Privacy

No logins. No accounts. No student data collected. Language Bridge is a static site with no server to send data to.

What we do not collect

  • No student accounts, names, or identifiers — student activity links never contain an identifier.
  • No third-party trackers, advertising, or analytics that identify individuals.
  • Nothing a student types — the ACE reflection prompts stay in the browser and are never stored or sent.

What stays on your device

Your interface-language and support-level preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them on your next visit. That information never leaves your device and you can clear it any time through your browser.

Works offline-friendly

Because there is no server and no tracking, activities load fast on a Chromebook and can be printed to use on paper when the network is unavailable.
