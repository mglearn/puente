About Language Bridge

Language Bridge is a free, privacy-first activity center for bilingual and ESL classrooms — not an online textbook and not a standards database with a few activities attached. Standards, frameworks, and teacher support wrap around useful student experiences.

It is built Texas-first (aligned to the ELPS and TEKS) but usable by any multilingual classroom. The design question every page answers: what can a teacher use with students in the next 10 minutes?

How it is built

  • No server, no accounts, no student data collected.
  • Works on a Chromebook — and on paper when the technology does not.
  • English and Spanish from the first release, with a language switcher and RTL support.
  • One reusable engine per activity type, fed validated content — not copy-pasted logic.
  • Official standards kept separate from local frameworks, and never over-attached.
