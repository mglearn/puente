Provenance & how it's made

Content, code, artwork, and the multilingual translations are produced with the help of generative-AI tools and refined under human editorial review — a human stays in the loop on every decision. The illustrations were generated with OpenAI (identified via embedded C2PA Content Credentials) and curated for this project; they are treated as content under the license above. Translations are machine-assisted and flagged for local educator review before classroom use.

Standards — the Texas ELPS and TEKS are the work of the Texas Education Agency, referenced here with plain-language summaries under fair educational use. Local frameworks (ACE, CLEAR) are the project's own and are labeled as such. No copyrighted textbook passages are used as content.
