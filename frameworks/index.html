Frameworks

Activities come first; frameworks wrap around them. Language Bridge is careful about what is official and what is ours.

Official vs. local. Texas ELPS and TEKS are official state standards. ACE and CLEAR are local instructional overlays authored for this project — they are not Texas Education Agency requirements. UDL 3.0 is CAST's framework, applied here as a design requirement, not a badge.
