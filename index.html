Language Bridge

Learn it. Connect it. Use it.

Bilingual and ESL activities, printables, and language supports for real classrooms — built for Texas K–12 educators and emergent bilingual students, and usable by any multilingual classroom.

No logins. No accounts. No student data collected. Works on a Chromebook, and works on paper when the technology does not.

This site is in development. Planned for the first release:

  • Cognate Detective — spot the connection, check the meaning, use the word
  • Academic Language Lab — say it, explain it, support it, use it
  • Newcomer Navigator — understand school, ask for help, join in
